# First 0–3-second clinical-safe hook options — v2.7.0

**Status:** original editorial design adapted from separately credited creator frameworks; there is **no promise of virality or retention rate**. Mobile text-only planning works; no CLI or extra video credits required.

## Contract
- Only AFTER the original case is read, FACT_REGISTER and exact source timeline exist, propose **three alternatives** for clip C01 (problem-first / question-first / clue-first). Every factual phrase, narrated historical claim, implied measurement and visible medical cue links to source FACT IDs. If a type has insufficient evidence, label `NO_SOURCE / REJECTED`, and offer a supported quieter opening instead.
- The first 0–3 seconds must connect the target audience, source-supported clinical situation and useful promise. Do not invent urgency or make up cliffhangers.
- Four optional layers: (A) first actual illustrated frame/action, (B) immediate short natural Bangla line or evidence-justified opening sound, (C) **optional editor-added** high-contrast short text for sound-off viewers, (D) approved diegetic sound/voice. **Do not ask the video model to render text inside STYLE01 frames.** Sound need not be sensational.
- The hook must belong to the final selected **8s C01 clip** or verified alternate unit, not an unbudgeted extra introduction. Avoid greetings/logo-card delay unless a clinically justified narrative genuinely calls for it.
- English 10–14 word advice is not a Bengali speech-length validator. Target natural short Bangla utterance, then pilot/measure actual voice length under the existing 7.0s (8s clip) or 8.5s (10s unit) spoken budget, including the first 3s scene objective.
- The question must be answered in the actual story with source-supported evidence, or explicitly identify unresolved diagnostic uncertainty. No false diagnosis, threatened death, invented false test, misquoted patient, fabricated count of doctors or manufactured treatment success.
- Humanizer may smooth EDITABLE dramatized Bengali only; protect quoted source, medication/clinical certainty, approved voice/asset locks and already approved C01/C09.

### Editorial review — not an analytics model

Mark each candidate `YES/NO/NOT_TESTED`: exact source support; audience can recognize the question; first 3s legible without relying solely on audio; wording naturally spoken by the right patient/doctor; promise is honestly fulfilled in this case; 8/10s clip duration feasible; no source/ethics/cast violations. Any critical NO -> REJECT/REPAIR; do not average into a bogus 'viral score'. Choose **one** after owner review; all other candidates remain unapproved drafts. If no credible hook can be built without inventing case history, state `HOOK_SOURCE_HOLD` and retain a truthful minimal opening.

### Clinically safe *conditional* examples only

**Not ready to paste into a case.** If original evidence really shows persistent symptoms after negative investigations, a fictionalized patient could say: "রিপোর্টে সমস্যা নেই বলেছে। তবু অস্বস্তি যাচ্ছে না।" If the source only documents symptoms and not test results, remove the result claim entirely. Avoid examples like "তিনজন ডাক্তার" or "এটাই রোগ" without documented count/confirmation.

### First-frame A/B without claims of controlled science

Compare 3 text-only openings at zero credits first. If owner authorizes a bounded actual test later, vary ONE selected hook element at a time, keep factual content/target audience/route settings constant where feasible, measure output and traffic sources; don't claim an actual randomised experiment or guaranteed reach. Previously accepted assets aren't automatically invalidated by creative exploration.
