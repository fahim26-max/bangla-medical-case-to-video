# Post-publication audience-retention feedback ledger — v2.7.0

**Nothing is measured until a creator supplies real analytics or a provider-authorized tool returns them.** This skill plans feedback, does not log into YouTube, Instagram or Meta, download CSV by itself, infer viewer behavior from video quality or promise virality. A 3–5 video review is an **optional pilot sample**, not a statistically reliable benchmark.

## Distinguish platform metrics

| Platform + surface | Early attention | Longer engagement | Interpretation limitation |
|---|---|---|---|
| YouTube **Shorts** | **Stayed to watch / Swiped away**: past initial seconds, **NOT an official fixed 3.0s cutoff** | Engaged views; average view duration and % viewed (engaged views denominators); available audience retention | Views may count starts/replays; compare dates/definitions and format |
| YouTube longer **Videos** | Intro = audience still watching after first 30s | Full video-level retention curve, dips/spikes/typical retention | Intro statistic must not be confused with a Shorts opening metric |
| Instagram **Reels** | Skip rate: skips inside first ~3s from initial views, if surfaced for account/API | Reels retention graph/watch time where available | Actual account access varies; estimated/developing metric, never infer ranking weight |
| Facebook Reels | Inspect the actual current creator dashboard's available watched/retention metrics | Platform-specific watched time/retention, if provided | Do not assert a Reel metric is identical to Instagram from name alone |
| TikTok | Check actual creator account's attention and retention panels | Watched time/completion if observed | TikTok Shop seller guidance is not proof of medical-video results |

**YouTube 2026 note:** YouTube Help describes broader starts/replays in views counting since 24 August 2026 while engaged-view and monetization treatment differ. Preserve actual report date and label, never compare incompatible definitions as if same metric.

## Evidence-driven editing loop
1. Only after real publication, collect platform-native measures over a recorded comparable reporting window and same content surface/format.
2. Confirm the first-second/three-second metric definition; **do not** equate YouTube Stayed to watch with Instagram's first-3-second Skip rate or YouTube 30-second Intro.
3. Identify largest observed audience exit/timecode (if chart available) and compare to exact script/beat map; treat correlation, audience distribution and title/thumbnail as potential confounders.
4. Propose **one editorial adjustment** to next NEW case's script/first frame; do not automatically change locked earlier clinical dialogue, asset masters or approved media.
5. Evaluate source/clinical mapping again and assess subsequent real performance; no universal thresholds, tiny-sample causality or fake A/B designation.
